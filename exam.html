The Scenario · GlobalTech Inc.

GlobalTech Inc., a 9,000-employee technology company, has grown for 12 years on a patchwork of spreadsheets and disconnected HR tools. Payroll runs out of a legacy system that team leads manually hand-enter every fortnight. Recruitment happens via three separate email inboxes. Performance reviews are annual Word documents that HR prints and files. The company has just hired you as an HRIS consultant. The CHRO wants a single, integrated HRIS and has asked you to recommend how to get there. Your task: Write a short consulting brief (Word document or PDF) that answers ALL of the following: 1. What core HR modules should an HRIS for GlobalTech include, and why does each matter to a company this size? 2. How would the lives of (a) an HR admin, (b) a team lead, and (c) an employee change if the HRIS replaced the current spreadsheets? 3. Which 3 risks or challenges (e.g. data migration, change management, data privacy) should the implementation plan handle, and how would you address each? 4. What would you put in a 6-month implementation roadmap? (phases, order, and why that order.) Structure your brief clearly (headings/sections), stay concise, and explain like you would to a colleague who is not from HR. Estimated length: 600–900 words.

Answer all 4 parts. Clarity and structure count as much as content.
